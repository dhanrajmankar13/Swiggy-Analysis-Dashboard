# Swiggy-Analysis-Dashboard

📊 Turning Data into Decisions: Swiggy Sales Dashboard Insights

Excited to share a snapshot of my latest analytics dashboard built around Swiggy sales data!

---

## 📊 Key Metrics (Top Row)

* **Total Sales:** ₹53.01M
* **Average Rating:** 4.34 ⭐
* **Rating Count:** 5.59M
* **Total Orders:** 197.43K
* **Avg Order Value:** ₹268.51

👉 Overall, strong ratings + high order volume suggest good customer satisfaction and steady demand.

---

## 📈 Sales Trends

### Monthly Sales Trend

* Sales fluctuate between **~6.2M and 6.9M**
* Peak around **May and August**
* Dip in **February**

👉 Likely seasonal effects or promotional campaigns influencing spikes.

---

### Daily Sales Trend

* Highest sales on **Saturday (7.8M)** and **Thursday (7.7M)**
* Lowest on **Monday/Tuesday (~7.4M)**

👉 Weekend demand is clearly stronger → good opportunity for targeted promotions.

---

### Weekly Trend

* Fairly stable pattern with minor fluctuations
* One noticeable dip at the end (possibly incomplete data)

---

## 🍽️ Sales Breakdown
